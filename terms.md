## 2. The Service

The App scans the photo library on the Apple device on which it is installed in order to surface candidate items for cleanup, including duplicate and visually similar photos, screenshots, burst photo extras, large files, videos, screen recordings, and Live Photos. The scan is performed locally on your device. The App then lets you review the candidates and:

- move selected items to the system **Hidden** album (the "**Safe Mode**" flow), or
- send selected items to the system **Recently Deleted** album, where the iOS Photos application controls the final deletion (the "**Advanced Mode**" flow).

The system prompts that ask you to confirm hiding, moving, or deleting items in your Photos library are displayed by iOS itself. The App does not bypass these prompts.

The App's user interface, the labels of "Premium" features, and the precise scope of free and paid functionality may change over time.
